About Me

Love to Do!!!

Beyond design, I'm fueled by curiosity. I love traveling, meeting people from different cultures, and exploring new cuisines. You'll also find me reading novels, playing chess, or enjoying a game of badminton - always learning, exploring, and finding inspiration in everyday experiences.

What I Do?

I design user-centered digital experiences by turning ideas into intuitive interfaces. From understanding user needs and mapping journeys to creating wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs, I focus on building products that are simple, accessible, and meaningful.

From Patents to Pixel

My journey began in patents, where I researched innovations, documented ideas, and learned to solve problems with precision and attention to detail.

While protecting innovations, I discovered my passion for designing meaningful user experiences. That curiosity led me to transition into UI/UX through an internship.
